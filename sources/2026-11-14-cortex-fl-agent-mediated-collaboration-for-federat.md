# Cortex-FL: Agent-Mediated Collaboration for Federated Learning

## 핵심 요약

Federated learning (FL) enables independent data owners to train models collaboratively without centralizing raw data. However, identifying collaborators, preparing training tasks, and coordinating execution and incentives hinder its practical adoption by organizations without specialized expertise. We present Cortex-FL, an agent-native FL system that enables users to express learning objectives in natural language and delegate the collaboration required to train a model.
