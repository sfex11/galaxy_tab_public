## 핵심 요약

An agent acting under partial observability must retain a recursively updateable statistic of history that restores the Markov property, but the smallest such statistic is generally unknown. We characterize this minimal Markov sufficient statistic for holonomy-cover decision processes, a structured POMDP class in which the visible dynamics are Markov and every realized visible transition applies a fixed permutation to a hidden mode.
