## 핵심 요약

Large language models (LLMs) trained on next-token prediction exhibit remarkable in-context learning (ICL) abilities, yet the representations that support ICL remain poorly understood. We consider such representations in a controlled setting: prompting LLMs with data emitted from hidden Markov models (HMMs) and probing for the corresponding belief state -- the posterior distribution over the HMM's hidden states given the observed token history.
