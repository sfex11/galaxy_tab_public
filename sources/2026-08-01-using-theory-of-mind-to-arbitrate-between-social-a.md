# Using Theory of Mind to Arbitrate between Social and Non-social Learning

**타입**: 논문  
**출처**: arXiv  
**날짜**: 2026-08-01  
**링크**: http://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28601v1

## 핵심 요약

Social learning is a powerful mechanism through which agents learn about the world from others. However, humans sometimes choose direct experience over social learning, which can carry time and cognitive resource costs. How do people balance social and non-social learning? We propose a Rational Mentalizing model of the decision to engage in social learning. This model estimates the utility of social learning by reasoning about another agent's goal and the informativeness of their future actions....
