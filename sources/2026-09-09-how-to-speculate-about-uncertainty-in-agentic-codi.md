## 핵심 요약

LLM agents deployed for software engineering fail expensively: they act confidently wrong, and bad actions are recognized only after costly execution and retry. We present Speculative Uncertainty (SU), a method that recovers a predictive failure signal for a black-box agent from its output tokens alone, with no access to logits, weights, activations, or repeated sampling.
