## 핵심 요약

Grammar-constrained decoding helps large language models produce syntactically valid structured outputs, such as code, JSON, and SQL. For context-free grammars, many practical decoders enforce local prefix feasibility: each token must keep the current prefix extendable to some valid completion. Yet, under tokenizer-grammar mismatch and finite token budgets, feasible prefixes may still fail to reach acceptance.
